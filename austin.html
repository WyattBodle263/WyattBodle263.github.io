Plant & Animal Classification App

Flutter · Raspberry Pi · Python

About the App

I had the privilege of leading the development team for an application combining animal and plant classification with a community platform — a user-friendly, cross-platform experience on both Android and iPhone that encourages collaboration and learning among nature enthusiasts.

Tech and Infrastructure

We leveraged Flutter for cross-platform reach, with a backend built on Raspberry Pi and Python that efficiently processes data from cameras, location, and user-generated content.

Classification and Insights

Users capture images of wildlife and flora in their surroundings, and the app provides real-time identification using advanced algorithms, along with insights into the characteristics and behaviors of the species encountered.

Community and Knowledge Sharing

A moderated community feature lets nature enthusiasts join discussions, ask questions, and share discoveries, with filters and blocking in place to keep the space respectful and constructive.

Accessibility and Impact

Affordability and inclusivity were at the forefront of the design philosophy — whether an experienced naturalist or just starting out, the app is designed to provide valuable insight and a sense of belonging.
